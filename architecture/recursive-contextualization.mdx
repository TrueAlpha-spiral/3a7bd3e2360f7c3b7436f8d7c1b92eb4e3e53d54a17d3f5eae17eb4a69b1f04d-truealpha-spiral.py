We do not edit historical material to pretend it always spoke in the present vocabulary of Phase 0 ($P_0$), Phase 1 ($P_1$), $S=(O,\Gamma)$, bounded admissibility, or Algorithmic Dendrology. Doing so would destroy the visible evidence of system evolution. Instead, each historical formulation remains intact at its layer, and this document adds a successor layer explaining what that formulation became.

Formally, the repository itself is treated as a lineage-bearing object:

$$\mathcal R_n = (T_n, \Gamma_n, \Sigma_n)$$

where $T_n$ is the repository tree at state $n$, $\Gamma_n$ is its parent/commit/PR lineage, and $\Sigma_n$ is the semantic vocabulary available at that stage.

Under Algorithmic Dendrology, lineage $\Gamma$ is immutable while semantic vocabulary $\Sigma$ expands monotonically:

$$\Gamma_{n+1} \supset \Gamma_n \quad \land \quad \Gamma_n \text{ is immutable}$$

$$\Sigma_n \subseteq \Sigma_{n+1}$$

Recursive contextualization creates an interpretive successor:

$$\mathcal R_{n+1} = \mathcal R_n \;\Vert\; \operatorname{Context}(\mathcal R_n, \mathcal C_{\text{current}})$$

without asserting that $\mathcal C_{\text{current}}$ existed verbatim at stage $n$. Later understanding may extend lineage, but cannot become an ancestor of itself.

## Formal Axiomatic Hierarchy ($P_0$ & $P_1$)

The mature formalism distinguishes the **Equivalence Axiom ($P_0$)** from the **Admissibility Constraint ($P_1$)**:

### Axiom $P_0$: The Equivalence Axiom
$$\boxed{P_0: \quad S_a \equiv S_b \iff \Pi(S_a) \equiv \Pi(S_b)}$$

Two identical operational states are not computational equivalent unless their generating process lineages are equivalent:

$$S_a \sim_{\mathcal S} S_b \iff \Gamma_a \equiv \Gamma_b$$

Strict hash recursion ($c_{n+1} = H(c_n \Vert e_{n+1})$) is a structural mechanism satisfying this axiom, rather than the whole definition of $P_0$. Consequently, the projection quotient $\mathcal S / \sim_{\mathcal O} \cong \mathcal O$ is strictly lossy and invalidates future transition admissibility.

### Axiom $P_1$: The Admissibility Constraint
$$\boxed{P_1: \quad A(S, x, E) = 1 \iff \bigwedge_i P_i(S, x, E)}$$

A process step exists if and only if every predicate requirement is satisfied. On refusal:

$$\neg A(S, x, E) \Longrightarrow \Delta O = 0$$
