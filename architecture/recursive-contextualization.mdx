## The Principle: $\text{Contextualize} \neq \text{Rewrite}$

We do not edit historical material to pretend it always spoke in the present vocabulary of Phase 0 ($P0$), Phase 1 ($P1$), $S=(O,\Gamma)$, bounded admissibility, or Algorithmic Dendrology. Doing so would destroy the visible evidence of system evolution. Instead, each historical formulation remains intact at its layer, and this document adds a successor layer explaining what that formulation became.

Formally, the repository itself is treated as a lineage-bearing object:

$$\mathcal R_n = (T_n, \Gamma_n, \Sigma_n)$$

where $T_n$ is the repository tree at state $n$, $\Gamma_n$ is its parent/commit/PR lineage, and $\Sigma_n$ is the semantic vocabulary available at that stage. Recursive contextualization creates an interpretive successor:

$$\mathcal R_{n+1} = \mathcal R_n \;\Vert\; \operatorname{Context}(\mathcal R_n, \mathcal C_{\text{current}})$$

without asserting that $\mathcal C_{\text{current}}$ existed verbatim at stage $n$.
