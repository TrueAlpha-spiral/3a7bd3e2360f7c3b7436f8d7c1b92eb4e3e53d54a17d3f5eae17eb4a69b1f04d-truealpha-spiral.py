## 3. The basic formula

A clean starting model:

`Recursive Compensation = Authorship Weight × Lineage Proximity × Derivative Value × Adoption Scale × Integrity Multiplier`

* **Derivative Value** = measurable commercial, institutional, technical, or strategic value created downstream
